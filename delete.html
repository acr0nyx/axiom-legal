Delete Your Axiom Account

Last updated: July 16, 2026

Axiom is provided by Acronyx. You can request deletion of your Axiom account and the data associated with it at any time.

How to request account deletion

In the Axiom app

  1. Open Axiom.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Review the deletion notice.
  5. Select Delete Account again to confirm.

Deletion initiated in the app normally begins immediately.

Without access to the app

Email acronyx.studio@gmail.com with the subject Axiom Account Deletion Request.

Send the request from the email address associated with your Axiom account. If you cannot access that address, include the account email in your request. Acronyx may request additional information to verify that you own the account. Never send your password.

Email deletion requests will normally be completed within 30 days. We will send confirmation after processing your request.

Data that is deleted

Deleting your account removes the following data from Axiom's active systems:

  • Your Axiom account and authentication credentials
  • Your email address and app profile
  • Linked sign-in identities
  • Premium entitlement records
  • Promo-code redemption records
  • Terms of Service and Privacy Policy acceptance records
  • Purchase identifiers stored in Axiom's active entitlement database

Calculator expressions and calculation history are processed locally and are not stored in your Axiom account on Acronyx's servers.

Data that may be retained temporarily

Some information may remain temporarily in restricted systems after your account is removed from active use:

  • Database backups: Deleted account data may remain in encrypted Supabase backups for up to 30 days, after which those backups expire under the applicable backup schedule.
  • Security and authentication logs: Limited records such as account identifiers, IP addresses, device information, login events, and deletion events may be retained for security, fraud prevention, and system auditing for up to 90 days.
  • Payment records: Google Play, Stripe, banks, or other payment processors may retain transaction and billing records for the period required by tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, or other legal obligations. These providers process retained records under their own privacy and retention policies.

Retained information is not used to restore your Axiom account or for advertising.

What happens after deletion

Account deletion is permanent. After processing:

  • You will no longer be able to sign in to the deleted account.
  • Premium access associated with that account will be removed.
  • Deleted account data cannot be restored from the app.
  • You may create a new account later, but it will not recover the deleted account's information or entitlements.
