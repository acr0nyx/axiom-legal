Privacy Policy for Axiom

Last updated: July 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Axiom ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our scientific calculator, graphing, geometry, and physics application.

Axiom is developed and operated by Acronyx. For purposes of this Privacy Policy, Acronyx is responsible for how your data is handled within the App, including account data, Supabase-based storage, premium entitlements, and promo codes. Questions about your data should be directed to the contact details in Section 9 below.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to operate the App:

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address, used solely for authentication and account access. Authentication and secure account management are provided through Supabase. Passwords are processed securely by Supabase and are not accessible to us.
  • Purchase Information: If you purchase Premium, which unlocks the Geometry and Physics tabs, the transaction is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your payment card details, billing address, or other financial information — Google handles this in accordance with its own privacy policy.
  • Promo Code Redemption: If you redeem a promo code, we record which code was used and the associated account, so we can track redemption and prevent reuse. We do not collect any additional personal information through this process.

Mathematical expressions, graph data, geometry inputs, and physics calculations entered into the App are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

Calculation history, preferences, and other calculator data are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to our servers.

We do not collect location data, device identifiers for advertising, contacts, or any other data beyond what is listed above.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Authenticate you when you log in
  • Verify and apply Premium purchases and promo code redemptions
  • Provide customer support if you contact us

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

Axiom relies on the following third-party services to function:

  • Supabase: Provides our backend infrastructure, including authentication and account data storage. Supabase processes data on our behalf in accordance with its own Privacy Policy and maintains its own security and privacy practices, available at supabase.com.
  • Google Play Billing: Processes all Premium purchases. Any payment information you provide is handled directly by Google, subject to Google's Privacy Policy, available at policies.google.com/privacy.

We do not use third-party advertising networks or analytics services that track you across other apps or websites.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your account data is stored securely using Supabase's infrastructure, which includes industry-standard security measures such as encryption in transit and access controls. While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You may delete your account and associated data at any time directly within the App, through your account settings. Once deleted, your account information is permanently removed from our systems, except where retention is required by law, such as records Google Play requires for completed purchase transactions, which are retained by Google, not by us.

6. Children's Privacy

Axiom is a general-purpose calculator, graphing, geometry, and physics tool intended for a general audience, including students, and is not specifically designed or marketed for children under 13. While the App may incidentally be used by younger students, it does not include content, characters, or features directed at young children, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13, or the relevant age of digital consent in your country, beyond what is necessary to create an account. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the email below and we will take steps to review and delete it as appropriate.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or delete your information, and the right to withdraw consent. You can exercise most of these rights directly in the App, such as account deletion, or by contacting us using the details below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this same location with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, contact us at: acronyx.tech@gmail.com.
